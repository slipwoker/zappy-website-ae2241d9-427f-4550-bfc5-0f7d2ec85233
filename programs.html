How We Measure Mastery & Enrich the Day

Mastery Scale

Understanding Our Grading Framework

At Ceretha Legacy Academy, progress is measured not by letter grades alone, but by genuine mastery. Our mastery-based model — powered by Prenda — ensures every student advances only when they have truly internalized a concept, building unshakeable academic foundations rooted in both biblical wisdom and mathematical precision.

4
Mastery Student can independently apply and extend the concept
3
Proficiency Student meets grade-level expectations with consistency
2
Developing Student shows partial understanding; targeted support continues
1
Beginning Student is introduced to the concept; intensive coaching applied
PBL Grading Categories

Project-Based Learning Assessment

Our project-based learning model in this Christian K–8 education setting evaluates students across four intentional dimensions, ensuring each child grows in character as well as competence.

  • Knowledge & Understanding Demonstrates comprehension of core content and biblical principles
  • Critical Thinking & Application Applies math reasoning and scripture to real-world challenges
  • Collaboration & Character Models servant leadership, teamwork, and faith-driven integrity
  • Presentation & Communication Articulates findings with clarity, confidence, and purpose

Bible principles and math reasoning are integrated into every real-world project, ensuring students connect academic learning to their God-given purpose.
