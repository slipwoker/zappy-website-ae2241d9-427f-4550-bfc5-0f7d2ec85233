Ceretha Legacy Academy — Academics

A Rigorous, Faith-Led Learning Experience for Grades K–8

At Ceretha Legacy Academy, every school day begins with purpose. Our Christian K–8 education model is built on the conviction that Bible and Math are foundational to daily instruction — equipping young minds not only with academic excellence, but with the wisdom and character to lead. Powered by Prenda’s mastery-based framework, our boutique microschool environment nurtures each child at their own pace within a structured, nurturing K–8 setting where no learner is left behind and no potential goes unnoticed.

Academics

Our Core Curriculum

At Ceretha Legacy Academy, every subject is taught with intention — weaving biblical truth and academic excellence into each lesson. Our Christian K–8 education is designed to equip young minds with the knowledge, character, and faith to lead purposefully.

Foundation

Bible

Scripture is at the heart of everything we do. Daily devotion and structured scripture study form the spiritual backbone of our school day — grounding students in God's Word and teaching them to see all of life through a biblical lens.

  • Daily devotion & morning worship
  • Scripture memorization & study
  • Biblical worldview integration
  • Character & leadership formation
"Train up a child in the way he should go." — Proverbs 22:6
Foundation

Mathematics

Mastery-based math instruction powered by Prenda ensures every student builds genuine understanding — not just procedural fluency. Our integrated math coaching model supports learners at every level, reinforcing the logical, ordered thinking that reflects God's design.

  • Mastery-based progression
  • Prenda-powered learning platform
  • Small-group math coaching
  • Real-world problem solving
Coaching: Mon / Wed / Thurs · 3:30–7:00 PM

English Language Arts

Developing confident communicators through reading comprehension, grammar, vocabulary, and literary analysis rooted in meaningful texts.

Science

Exploring God's creation through inquiry-based learning, experiments, and discovery — nurturing curiosity and wonder about the natural world.

Social Studies

Understanding history, civics, and culture through a biblical lens — helping students become informed, principled citizens who honor God in community.

Reading Lab

Targeted, small-group reading instruction designed to accelerate fluency, phonics mastery, and comprehension for every learner at their level.

Writing Lab

Building strong writers through structured composition, creative expression, and the development of a clear, purposeful voice across all genres.

STEM

Hands-on science, technology, engineering, and math projects that challenge students to design, build, and solve — reflecting God's call to create and steward.

Art

Nurturing creativity as a gift from God — students explore color, form, and expression through visual arts that celebrate beauty and cultivate imagination.

Physical Education

Honoring the body as a temple — students develop fitness, teamwork, sportsmanship, and a lifelong appreciation for health and physical well-being.

Leadership

Rooted in servant-leadership principles, this course equips students to lead with integrity, empathy, and purpose — raising the next generation of kingdom-minded leaders.

Our Daily Rhythm

A Day at Ceretha Legacy Academy

Our faith-centered K–8 microschool follows a purposeful daily schedule designed to nurture mastery, character, and leadership. Every hour is intentional — grounded in biblical principles and academic excellence.

8:00 – 8:30 AM

Morning Devotion

We open each day in prayer and scripture. Bible study is foundational — not supplemental — to our Christian K–8 education.

8:30 AM – 12:00 PM

Core Instruction

Mastery-based Math, ELA, Science, Social Studies, Reading Lab, and Writing Lab — taught with rigor and purpose through our Prenda-powered learning model.

12:00 – 12:45 PM

Lunch & Recess

Rest, nourishment, and play — essential rhythms that restore focus and build community among our students.

12:45 – 1:45 PM

STEM, Art & PE

Hands-on learning in STEM, Art, Physical Education, and Leadership — developing the whole child with creativity, movement, and purpose.

1:45 – 3:00 PM

Enrichments & Clubs

After core day, students explore enrichment clubs and leadership programming — extending learning beyond the classroom in a faith-based microschool community.

Math Coaching Continues

Mastery-based math coaching extends on designated afternoons and evenings — Monday, Wednesday & Thursday, 3:30–7:00 PM. Included for microschool students; available to traditional students as an enrichment investment. Because math reasoning is central to every real-world project we tackle.

Mon · Wed · Thu 3:30 – 7:00 PM

Our Learning Model

A Foundation Built to Last

Mastery-Based Progression

Every student advances only when they truly understand — not just when the calendar says so. Our mastery-based math coaching, powered by Prenda, ensures each child builds on a rock-solid foundation before moving forward, eliminating gaps and building genuine confidence.

Individualized Support & Small-Group Care

As a boutique faith-based microschool, our intentionally small enrollment means your child is truly known — by name, by strength, and by calling. Instruction is tailored to each learner's pace, learning style, and God-given potential within a nurturing, purpose-driven environment.

Leadership Formation Through Purpose-Driven Instruction

Guided by Proverbs 22:6, every lesson — from Bible devotion to STEM projects — is designed to raise leaders who think with clarity and serve with conviction. Biblical principles and math reasoning are woven into real-world projects, shaping students who are academically excellent and spiritually grounded.
