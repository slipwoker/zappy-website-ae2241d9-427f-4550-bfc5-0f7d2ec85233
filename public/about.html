Our Mission & Vision

Raising Leaders.
Restoring Purpose.

Ceretha Legacy Academy is a faith-centered Christian K–8 microschool in Haines City, FL — a place where rigorous academics and spiritual formation walk hand in hand. We believe every child is called to greatness, and our purpose is to equip them with the knowledge, character, and confidence to fulfill that calling. Rooted in biblical principles and powered by mastery-based learning, we nurture the whole child: mind, heart, and spirit.

Founded by Carrie Riley, our academy blends the warmth of a boutique learning community with the intentionality of a rigorous academic program. Bible and Math are foundational to our daily instruction — not as separate subjects, but as lenses through which every lesson is understood. From project-based learning rooted in scripture to personalized math coaching, every element of our school is designed to raise up the next generation of leaders.

Meet the Founder

Carrie Riley

Founder & Lead Educator, Ceretha Legacy Academy

Carrie Riley founded Ceretha Legacy Academy with a singular conviction: that every child deserves an education rooted in purpose, faith, and academic excellence. Her journey into Christian K–8 education was shaped by years of observing the gap between what children are capable of and what traditional schooling often offers — and a deep calling to bridge that gap for families in Haines City, FL.

Guided by Proverbs 22:6 — "Train up a child in the way he should go, and when he is old he will not depart from it" — Carrie built a learning environment that is both rigorous and nurturing. Through the Prenda microschool model, she has created a boutique-luxury academic experience where mastery-based math coaching, project-based learning, and daily biblical devotion are woven into every lesson.

Carrie believes that raising leaders begins with intentionality — in the curriculum, in the classroom, and in the hearts of the educators who show up every day. She is committed to serving families who seek values-driven education that doesn't ask them to choose between faith and academic rigor.

Our Foundation

Pillars of a Purposeful Education

At Ceretha Legacy Academy, every decision — from curriculum to culture — is guided by timeless principles. These four pillars shape our learning environment and define how we form the whole child: academically, spiritually, and personally.

Faith First

Scripture is not a supplement — it is the foundation. Daily Bible devotion and scripture study are woven into every subject, anchoring our students in truth and equipping them to see all of life through a biblical lens. Proverbs 22:6 guides every step.

Proverbs 22:6

Academic Excellence

We hold every student to a standard of mastery, not just completion. Through Prenda's mastery-based model, rigorous math coaching, and project-based Christian learning, students build genuine competence and the confidence to lead. Mediocrity is never the goal.

Mastery-Based Learning

Raising Leaders

We are not simply raising students — we are raising the next generation of servant leaders. Through enrichment clubs, project-based challenges, and a culture of responsibility, every child at Ceretha Legacy Academy is shaped with the character and vision to make a lasting impact.

Character Formation

Family Partnership

Education is a covenant between school and home. We partner closely with families who share our values and commitment to intentional, purposeful upbringing. Open communication, transparent progress, and shared vision are the hallmarks of every family relationship we build.
