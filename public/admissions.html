Step Up UA PEP

Florida Scholarship Programs Accepted

We proudly accept the Step Up for Students Unique Abilities (Step Up UA) and Personal Education Program (PEP) scholarships — two of Florida's most impactful education choice programs. These scholarships can cover tuition, making our rigorous, faith-led microschool model accessible to families across Haines City and Polk County.

Whether your child qualifies through unique learning needs or income eligibility, our admissions team will walk you through every step of the scholarship application process with care and clarity.

  • Step Up UA — for students with unique learning needs
  • PEP — income-based scholarship for qualifying families
  • Dedicated support through every step of the application
  • Serving grades K–8 in Haines City, FL
