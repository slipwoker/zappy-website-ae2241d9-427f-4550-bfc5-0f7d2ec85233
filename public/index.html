Why Ceretha Legacy Academy

A Rigorous, Nurturing Environment for K–8 Students

Ceretha Legacy Academy is more than a school — it is a calling. We offer Christian K–8 education rooted in biblical truth, where every subject, every project, and every relationship is guided by purpose. In intentional partnership with families, we raise leaders who are academically prepared and spiritually grounded.

  • Faith-Centered Instruction

    Every lesson is anchored in biblical truth. Bible is a core daily subject — not an add-on — shaping how our students understand the world, themselves, and their purpose. Guided by Proverbs 22:6, we train up each child in the way they should go.

  • Mastery-Based Math Coaching

    Math is not just taught — it is mastered. Powered by Prenda, our integrated math coaching model ensures each student advances only when they truly understand the material. Coaching sessions run Monday, Wednesday, and Thursday evenings for deeper skill-building.

  • Project-Based Learning & Leadership

    Our project-based christian school model brings learning to life through real-world challenges rooted in biblical principles and mathematical reasoning. Students develop critical thinking, collaboration, and the kind of character that defines true leaders.
